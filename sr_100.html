84 Sample Runners

Eighty-four ready-to-use runners, contacts and rivals, grouped into teams for Shadowrun, Fifth Edition.

Load them into your sheets. All 84 runners on this page can go straight into the Character Sheets: press Load sample characters there, or download the sample sheet file and use Import. Each sheet has the street name, metatype and any attributes the card gives (so the limits, initiatives and condition monitors work themselves out once enough are filled in), with the card's gear sorted onto the sheet: weapons, cyberware and bioware with their Essence where the rating is known, cyberdecks, armor, spells, adept powers, spirits, sprites and complex forms, contacts, SINs and the rest under Gear, Vehicles & Drones. The specialty goes under Specializations, and the role, background and anything else under Notes. Team members get their team's name, reputation and the rest of its roster under Notes. Most cards give few or no numbers, so add the missing attributes, Edge, skill ratings and weapon and armor stats from the core rulebook.

Building Your Own Characters

This collection of 84 characters demonstrates the incredible diversity possible in Shadowrun. Each character shows how the core archetypes can be customized with unique backgrounds, specializations, and personal motivations. Use these examples as inspiration for creating your own runners, or drop them directly into your campaigns as NPCs, contacts, rivals, or pre-generated player characters.

Key Patterns to Notice:

  • Background Drives Behavior: Each character's corporate, military, academic, or street background shapes their approach to shadowrunning
  • Specialization Within Archetypes: Street Samurai can be snipers, brawlers, or defensive specialists
  • Cross-Archetype Teams: Successful teams combine different skills and approaches
  • Personal Stakes: The best characters have reasons beyond money for their shadowrunning
  • Interconnected Stories: Characters with compatible or conflicting backgrounds create natural drama

Using This Collection:

  • Player Characters: Pick any character as a starting point for player creation
  • NPCs: Use as contacts, rivals, or supporting characters
  • Team Templates: The complete teams provide ready-made opposition or allied groups
  • Campaign Seeds: Each character's background suggests potential storylines
  • Inspiration: Mix and match elements to create entirely new characters

Remember: The best Shadowrun characters are shaped by the world's unique blend of cyberpunk technology and fantasy magic, corporate power and street-level desperation. Let these contradictions create interesting internal conflicts and external challenges for every character you create.
