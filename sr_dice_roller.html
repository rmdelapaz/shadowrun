Dice Roller

Roll any d6 pool with hits, limits, glitches and Edge, see the exact odds, then resist the damage and track it.

Set your pool, then roll.

Exact Odds

Worked out from every way the dice can land, not by simulation. Each die is a hit one time in three, so a pool averages a third of its size in hits before the limit bites.

ResultChance

Glitch: more than half the dice show 1 (exactly half is not a glitch). Critical glitch: a glitch with no hits. With Push the Limit the 1s are counted on the dice you first roll, Edge dice included, not on the extra dice from exploding 6s. Second Chance rerolls every die that missed once; it cannot undo a glitch, so the glitch odds are those of the first roll.

Resist Damage

Modified DV = weapon DV + the attacker's net hits. Armor is modified by AP (AP −1 takes 1 off Armor). If the modified DV is less than the modified Armor the damage is Stun instead of Physical. You roll Body + modified Armor, and every hit takes 1 off the DV. If modified Armor is 0 or less, you roll Body alone. Damage resistance has no limit.

Physical boxes = 8 + half your Body, rounded up; Stun boxes = 8 + half your Willpower, rounded up. Every full row of 3 filled boxes is −1 die to your tests and −1 to Initiative, and the two tracks add together. A full Stun track knocks you out, and extra Stun spills onto the Physical track at 1 box for every 2. A full Physical track means you are dying; overflow beyond your Body in boxes means death.

Initiative

Initiative Score = Reaction + Intuition + 1D6, plus up to four more dice from augmentations, spells or hot-sim (5D6 at most). The highest score acts first. When everyone has acted, every score drops by 10, and anyone still above 0 acts again in the next pass. Wound modifiers come off the score.
